About

I am Ajay Joshi, a CA Finalist with a strong interest in taxation, audit and business consultancy.

I enjoy understanding the practical side of finance, regulatory compliance and business operations. My work is driven by continuous learning, analytical thinking and a commitment to helping businesses handle their financial and compliance challenges effectively.

Audit

My core areas are bank audits and GST compliance. In the banking sector I have been involved in concurrent audits, bank branch audits, statutory audits and stock audits, with practical exposure to internal controls, regulatory compliance, risk assessment and financial verification.

Indirect taxation (GST)

My main focus has been GST return filing and refunds. I have also worked on GST audits, input tax credit reconciliations, GST reversals, e-invoice compliance, e-way bill management and day-to-day GST requirements.

Company and corporate compliance

Exposure to statutory audits of private limited companies and compliance under the Companies Act, 2013, including company incorporation and MCA filings such as DIR-3 KYC, MGT-7, AOC-4, INC-20A, XBRL and LLP compliances. I also assist with startup and Udyam (MSME) registration.

Direct taxation

Income Tax return filing for individuals and businesses, along with TDS/TCS compliance, return filing, reconciliation and related advisory matters.

Common questions

What documents do I need to start?

It depends on the work. For most matters we ask for your PAN, Aadhaar, relevant registrations and any notice or records involved. Call or message us and we will give you an exact list.

How soon will you respond?

We aim to respond promptly to every call, WhatsApp message and email. If you have a deadline, mention it in your first message.

I received a GST or Income Tax notice. What should I do?

Do not ignore it, and note the reply date. Send us a copy and we will review what it says and explain the next steps.

Can you help me register a company, LLP or startup?

Yes. We guide you on the right structure, prepare the documents and handle the registration, including Udyam (MSME) and startup registration, and the compliance that follows.

Do you work with clients outside Ahmedabad and Palanpur?

Yes. Most work can be done online, with documents shared digitally and discussions held by phone or video call.

How are your fees decided?

Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. We discuss and agree on fees with you before we begin.

Do you handle regular monthly compliance?

Yes. We can manage monthly GST, TDS and bookkeeping so nothing is missed.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Client information is treated as confidential and used only for your work.
